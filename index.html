    How to play
    
  
    You are auditing a ledger. Some entries are forged, the rest are genuine. The number of forgeries is shown at the top.
    
  
    Every entry carries a note written by whoever made it. Genuine entries tell the truth. Forged entries lie — their note is always false.
    
  
    A few entries have already been audited: you can read their notes. Use them to work out whether another entry must be genuine or forged, then tap it and judge it. If you are right, its note opens and gives you more to work with. If you are wrong, that counts as a mistake (the correct answer is revealed).
    
  
    There is never any guessing needed: at every moment at least one entry can be proven. Tap an audited entry and the entries its note talks about are outlined in blue. Keyboard: arrows to move, G genuine, F forged, [ ] previous / next day.
    
  
    Missed a day? Use ‹ › next to the date to play earlier ledgers. Archive ledgers count in your statistics but not in your streak.
    
  
    “This row”, “this column”, “above”, “below” refer to the note's own entry. Rows are numbered 1–6, columns A–F. Corners are the four corner entries of the ledger.

    Puzzle packs
    
  
    A pack is a set of extra ledgers that never appear as daily puzzles. The daily ledger stays free. Load a pack file (.json) you downloaded; it is kept only in this browser.
    
  
    Pack ledgers have a Hint button (H): it points at an entry you can prove next and outlines the notes that prove it, without telling you the answer. Hints show in your result (🟨).
